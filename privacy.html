Privacy Policy

Effective 24 August 2026

Flipworthy is built to keep your data on your phone. This page explains exactly what leaves it, and why.

Photos you scan

When you tap Analyze, the photos you chose are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards them to an AI vision provider (currently Google's Gemini API) to identify the item and draft a listing. We do not store your photos on our servers. They are processed for that request and discarded; the provider handles them under its API terms. The copies you see in the app are saved only on your device.

Your inventory

Scan history, listings, prices, costs and profit figures are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

A device identifier

Flipworthy generates a random identifier and keeps it in your device's secure Keychain. It is sent with each scan so we can enforce the free-scan limit and protect the service from abuse. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID, and it is not used for advertising or tracking.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings at any time.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Accuracy

Identifications and price estimates are generated by an AI model and may be wrong. They are estimates to help you research, not guarantees of value or authenticity. Always verify against real sold listings before pricing a high-value item.

Children

Flipworthy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
